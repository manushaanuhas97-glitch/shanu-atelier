# Craft & Decor Online Store Website 🎨✨

Handcrafted Jesmonite & Plaster Decor e-commerce website with interactive Cart & WhatsApp Direct Ordering.

## 📁 Project Structure:
- index.html - The main website home page & store.
- *.jpg - High-resolution studio product photos (Tray, Shell dish, Lotus holder, Candle jar).
- molds_shopping_guide.html - Silicone mold purchasing guide with Daraz & AliExpress direct links.
- 	rending_molds_sri_lanka.html - Top selling craft molds showcase.

## 🛠️ How to Customize:
1. **Change WhatsApp Number:**
   Open index.html in VS Code, search for 94770000000, and replace it with your real Sri Lankan WhatsApp number (e.g. 9471xxxxxxx or 9477xxxxxxx).

2. **Change Store Name:**
   Search for AURA STONE in index.html and replace it with your own brand name.

3. **Change Product Prices:**
   Locate the items array in <script> near the bottom of index.html and adjust price or 	itle.

## 🚀 How to Run:
- Right-click index.html inside VS Code and select **'Open with Live Server'**, or double-click index.html to open directly in your web browser!
